# UX/UI audit — 7 September 2026

Status: completed review of the current development UI; implementation has not started.

## Assessment

Keenti has a coherent visual foundation and substantially more planning functionality than the earlier conversation initially recognized. Its main weakness is helping someone interpret their position and choose the next action. Existing features are divided by data type, while the user's questions cross Accounts, Boxes, Transactions, Subscriptions, and Debts.

The priority is to make the information trustworthy and connect the existing workflows. A new forecast should follow an agreed calculation model, rather than replacing Available to Spend with an unexplained number.

## What works and should be retained

- Consistent spacing, cards, rounded controls, colors, readable currency amounts, and a recognizable visual identity.
- Account overview separates assets and credit; Box copy explains that reserving money does not create a Transaction.
- Transactions and Debts adapt to cards on mobile. Mobile navigation has visible labels and a working More menu.
- Forms expose exact Box funding and an immediate projected Available to Spend. In the sample draft, a MXN 100 expense lowered the projection from −760.25 to −860.25; explicitly funding all 100 from a Box restored the projection to −760.25.
- Saving Goals, Spending Budgets, Funding Triggers, and historical Box activity already exist. Plan copy explicitly says money is not moved automatically.
- Disabled Box deposits explain the current allocation shortfall. Account archival also explains its balance prerequisite.
- The long mobile Transaction dialog can be scrolled to reveal its final balance summary and actions; those fields are not permanently inaccessible.

## Findings

Priority: **P1** = misleading financial information or a material task obstacle; **P2** = significant friction or discoverability; **P3** = secondary consistency and polish. These indicate remediation order, not financial loss demonstrated by the audit.

### UX-01 · P1 · Balance meaning is incomplete and one explanation is wrong

**Observed:** Dashboard shows Net Balance 2,889.75, In Boxes 3,650.00, and Available to Spend −760.25. It describes Net Balance as “Ingresos históricos menos egresos.” Accounts shows 2,817.75 in accounts plus 72.00 credit in favor, totaling 2,889.75.

**Source-confirmed:** After account tracking activates, Net Balance uses signed account balances. Available to Spend subtracts current Box balances. Neither is a separate forecast of future bills. The actual current formula is correct under ADR-0022; its explanatory copy is stale.

**Impact:** Someone can reasonably read the headline as immediately spendable cash, despite credit balances, reserved amounts, and unplanned future costs. Opening balances also make lifetime income-minus-expenses an unreliable explanation.

**Recommendation:** Explain the calculation using the actual tracking mode. Provide a visible balance breakdown that distinguishes money held, credit owed/credit in favor, and Box reservations. State that upcoming costs are not separately deducted. Preserve the canonical Net Balance and Available to Spend definitions; any forecast needs its own label and model.

**Evidence:** `/`, `/accounts`; `frontend/src/routes/+page.svelte`, `frontend/messages/es.json`, `backend/.../application/service/DashboardService.java`; ADR-0022.

### UX-02 · P1 · “Reconciliation” diagnoses too much from one negative number

**Observed:** Dashboard, global balance strip, Boxes overview, and Box detail repeatedly direct the user to reconcile. The overview suggests withdrawing from Boxes, recording missing income, or correcting a Transaction.

**Impact:** A negative unallocated balance could mean over-allocation, an overall negative net position, or incorrect/missing records. The UI does not distinguish them. In this dev fixture, Box reserves exceed the positive Net Balance by 760.25; the displayed amount alone does not establish a banking shortfall or recording error.

**Recommendation:** Explain the condition before proposing an action: “Your Box reservations exceed your net balance by …” when that is supported. Offer a breakdown and relevant actions. When a Box is empty, do not recommend withdrawing from that Box. Retain blocking of new deposits and acceptance of real expenses.

**Evidence:** `/`, `/boxes`, `/boxes/1`; `frontend/src/routes/boxes/[id]/+page.svelte`; ADR-0020 and ADR-0022. An empty Box warning was reported earlier but was not reproduced with this funded dev fixture.

### UX-03 · P1 · Subscription linking describes expenses but accepts income

**Observed:** Shared subscription → “Vincular movimientos” says “Selecciona gastos sin vincular…”, but its candidates are positive income records, including a salary and a debt payment.

**Source-confirmed:** The page filters candidates to INGRESS. The backend also rejects non-INGRESS links. This is not just the wrong records being returned accidentally.

**Impact:** A user trying to attach the provider's charge cannot complete the stated task. A user collecting contributions is given the wrong explanation. Relabeling everything as expenses would conflict with current behavior.

**Recommendation:** First clarify the existing action as linking received income/contributions. Then design provider charges and member contributions as distinct concepts. Supporting expense links requires an explicit contract change across UI, backend, and tests.

**Evidence:** `/subscriptions/1` → linking dialog; `frontend/src/routes/subscriptions/[id]/+page.server.ts:109`; `backend/.../application/service/TransactionService.java:242`.

### UX-04 · P1 · Past obligations are called “next,” without a clear status model

**Observed:** On 7 September, subscription cards show “Próximo cobro” for 15, 20, and 25 August. Credit detail calls 20 August “Próximo pago,” with 40.00 outstanding, while also showing 72.00 credit in favor and a current estimate of 0.00.

**Impact:** Users must infer whether a date is overdue, stale, paid but unrecorded, or waiting for billing generation. Credit account balance and confirmed statement obligations can legitimately differ; the UI does not explain the distinction. The audit does not conclude that the displayed 40.00 is an actual bank debt.

**Recommendation:** Distinguish overdue, upcoming, pending generation, and recorded/confirmed states. Explain current credit balance versus confirmed statement obligations, and link to the action that resolves the specific state. Derive relative dates using the user's time zone.

**Evidence:** `/subscriptions`, `/subscriptions/1`, `/accounts/2`, `/`; ADR-0019 confirms billing generation is manual and can leave the stored billing date behind today. Do not fix this by silently advancing billing dates.

### UX-05 · P1 · Debt progress is visually full at 0%

**Observed:** `/debts/1` shows total 900.00, paid 0.00, remaining 900.00, and “Progreso 0%,” with a fully green bar. Accessibility reports progress 0.

**Source-confirmed:** The page applies `bg-green-500` to the Progress root/track.

**Recommendation:** Use a neutral track and colored filled indicator. Verify 0%, partial, and 100% visually as well as semantically.

**Evidence:** `frontend/src/routes/debts/[id]/+page.svelte:139`, `frontend/src/lib/components/ui/progress/progress.svelte`.

### UX-06 · P2 · Planning is hidden behind a history label

**Observed:** Each Box card's main action is “Ver historial.” Its destination contains planning, allocation controls, Funding Triggers, and history. A no-plan Box has two “Crear plan” buttons. Overview cards do not communicate that planning exists.

**Impact:** Users can miss working features and resort to separate notes or manual withdrawals. The earlier claim that planning was absent was incorrect.

**Recommendation:** Make the Box itself a clear destination (“Open Box” / “Abrir Caja”), show its plan status or “Add a plan,” and use one primary setup action. Organize detail into plan, money allocation, and history with explicit headings. Surface spending-from-Box near relevant actions.

**Evidence:** `/boxes`, `/boxes/1`; `frontend/src/lib/components/boxes/box-card.svelte`; `frontend/src/routes/boxes/[id]/+page.svelte`.

### UX-07 · P2 · Plan configuration exposes too many mechanics too early

**Observed:** Saving Goal setup asks for target, date, cadence, day-of-month, and optional regular commitment. Before meaningful inputs, it says approximately 0.00 over 0 periods. Trigger UI alternates “activador” and “disparador.”

**Impact:** A simple goal feels like configuring a schedule. The zero preview looks like a result before a result is available. “Saving suggestions” also undersells that Spending Budgets can receive funding.

**Recommendation:** Begin with purpose, amount, and date (or amount and recurrence for a budget). Reveal schedule customization progressively. Show a meaningful preview only after required inputs are present. Use “When income arrives…” wording and “suggested Box deposits”; retain confirmation before applying money movements.

**Evidence:** `/boxes/1` → Create plan (both types), Add trigger; ADR-0021. Active-plan progress and revision states still need fixture-based verification.

### UX-08 · P2 · Transaction recording mixes essential entry with optional allocation

**Observed:** Direction is a dropdown defaulting to income. Switching to expense leaves a salary example in Description. Adding a Box row immediately shows “amount must be greater than 0” and selects the first Box. Funding and the projected balance are far down the dialog. The list exposes three ordering controls but no search or meaningful financial filters.

**Impact:** Recording a common purchase takes interpretation, and optional funding may be missed. Automatic first-category/first-Box choices can be mistaken for an intelligent recommendation. Reviewing past activity becomes harder as the list grows.

**Recommendation:** Offer clear income/expense choices; adapt examples and labels. Keep amount, date, account, and category prominent. Make “Use reserved Box money” easy to discover, with exact optional splits and a readable consequence summary. Delay errors until interaction or submission. Add search, date, account, category, and direction filters before adding more presentation controls.

**Evidence:** `/transactions` desktop/mobile, unsaved expense and funding draft; transaction allocation components.

### UX-09 · P2 · Debt payment entry does not explain the resulting income record

**Observed:** A freshly opened unpaid debt shows a required-category error before interaction. It offers “Registrar pago” and “Cuenta receptora,” without explaining that an income Transaction is created. The overall page says debts are for embroidery jobs, while the fixture describes shared repairs.

**Source-confirmed:** A Debt Payment creates one INGRESS Transaction. “Deudas” can also be read as debts the user owes, although the overview's “Te debe” helps clarify the direction.

**Recommendation:** Use “Money owed to you” / “Te deben” as the user-facing framing, subject to product review. State the save effect beside the action and link the resulting Transaction after success. Neutralize the form until touched/submitted and avoid hard-coded work-specific introductory copy unless this is deliberately the product scope.

**Evidence:** `/debts`, `/debts/1`; ADR-0005; `frontend/src/routes/debts/[id]/+page.server.ts:98`.

### UX-10 · P2 · Subscription detail has separate records without a period summary

**Observed:** Detail stacks Members, Linked Transactions, and Payment Records. A shared subscription with zero members says there are no members but provides no local “Add members” action. Monthly/annual totals show full subscription prices without a gross-versus-own-share explanation.

**Impact:** The user must assemble provider cost, contributions, and their own portion. The current fixture cannot verify populated split behavior, but the empty-state dead end and absent summary are visible.

**Recommendation:** Present the current billing period, provider cost, expected contributions, collected amount, outstanding amount, and own share with clearly defined meanings. Separate linking existing income from marking a member's record paid. Provide an Add members action where the empty state appears. Label aggregate prices as gross until a net-cost model is agreed.

**Evidence:** `/subscriptions`, `/subscriptions/1`. Populated member/payment states remain a validation requirement, not a confirmed defect.

### UX-11 · P2 · Navigation prioritizes destinations inconsistently

**Observed:** Desktop dock uses icons with hover labels. Mobile has three labeled pinned links and More; the inspected configuration pins Transactions, Subscriptions, and Debts, hiding Dashboard, Boxes, and Accounts. Settings supports customization. Outside Dashboard, the mobile balance strip displays only an alert icon and −760.25, with its meaning available in the accessibility label but not visible text.

**Impact:** First-time discovery depends on guessing icons or exploring More. The balance strip emphasizes an unexplained negative number precisely where context is most limited.

**Recommendation:** Keep persistent text labels on desktop; give Dashboard a stable mobile entry and review Box prominence. Preserve deliberate user pin choices during migration. Show a concise visible label for the global balance or offer an expandable breakdown.

**Evidence:** Desktop screenshots; mobile More menu and Settings navigation preferences. This is an observed configuration, not a claim about every user's defaults.

### UX-12 · P2 · Mobile layouts need targeted repairs

**Observed:** At 390px the Dashboard credit alert's “Revisar cuenta” button overlaps the long account-name title. The debt heading/description is squeezed alongside two actions. Contacts and Trash retain wide tables, pushing actions and important fields beyond the initial view; Transactions and Debts already use cards.

**Source-confirmed:** Alert actions are positioned absolutely while text gets a fixed right padding, which does not account for variable label width.

**Recommendation:** Stack alert actions under text on narrow screens. Allow page-header actions to wrap beneath titles. Adapt Contacts and Trash with mobile cards or a clear, keyboard-accessible scroll treatment and persistent action access. Validate long localized text, not only short English fixtures.

**Evidence:** `/` at 390px, `/debts`, `/contacts`, `/trash`; `frontend/src/lib/components/ui/alert/alert-action.svelte`, `alert.svelte`.

### UX-13 · P2 · Credit detail emphasizes administration before the current task

**Observed:** Above the fold, credit detail dedicates large cards to tracking start and credit limit and prominently explains why archival is disabled. Statement confirmation, confirmed statements, MSI setup, and activity extend the page. No direct “Record card payment” action is visible in the reviewed account detail; the overview offers a generic Transfer action.

**Recommendation:** Lead with the current position, next/overdue obligation, and the appropriate record-payment/review-statement action. Put archival and rarely changed settings behind a secondary menu. Explain that recording a card payment is an internal Transfer and does not create another expense.

**Evidence:** `/accounts`, `/accounts/2`; ADR-0022. The underlying Transfer workflow was not submitted.

### UX-14 · P1 · Source risk: failed loading can look like zero money or no records

**Source-only finding:** Dashboard initializes an all-zero summary and retains it when fetching the real summary fails. Subscription detail initializes empty arrays and can return them after secondary requests fail. The UI receives no explicit per-section failure status from those paths.

**Impact:** A service failure can be presented as a legitimate zero balance or empty membership/payment history. This was not triggered in the live browser and must not be described as a witnessed outage.

**Recommendation:** Distinguish loading, empty, failed, and stale states. Financial totals should say unavailable on failure; successful sections may remain usable with a partial-data notice and Retry.

**Evidence:** `frontend/src/routes/+page.server.ts:24–70`, `frontend/src/routes/subscriptions/[id]/+page.server.ts:59–106`.

## Implications for the earlier conversation

- Confirmed: confusing balance language, over-broad reconciliation warnings, hidden planning, duplicate plan calls to action, technical setup language, misleading debt progress, and weak feature-to-feature connections.
- Corrected: Box spending does not always require a separate withdrawal. Explicit funding is present and its draft preview works. Planning is not wholly missing. Account tracking changes Net Balance's source.
- Newly established: stale “next” dates, income-only subscription links described as expenses, immediate form errors, a mobile alert overlap, and failure-state risks in source.
- Not established: production state, the user's actual financial shortfall, an empty-Box warning in this dev dataset, the exact populated shared-subscription experience, or automatic monthly recording of provider expenses. Manual Payment Record generation is not evidence of an automatically created EGRESS Transaction.

## Recommended product direction

The dashboard should help the user answer: what is recorded now, what money is reserved, what needs attention, and what is expected but not yet received. Keep historical charts accessible below these decisions. Build that view from the existing ledgers and plans first.

For irregular income, a configurable planning horizon is more appropriate than assuming a payday. A proposed 30-day view must disclose missing essentials and distinguish recorded obligations from estimates. Credit settlement and provider charges must not be subtracted twice; expected income must never silently become spendable money.
